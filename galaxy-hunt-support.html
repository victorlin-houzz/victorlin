Galaxy Hunt! Support

A little help for your next constellation.

Contact the developer

Email Victor Lin at swissashley@gmail.com with "Galaxy Hunt! Support" in the subject.

For a bug report, include the app version shown in Settings, your device model and iOS version, what happened, and what you expected. A screenshot can help; remove any personal information before sending it. You do not need to provide a password, account identifier, or payment details.

Progress and saved games

Your game is saved on your device. Galaxy Hunt! has no developer-operated account or cloud save, so we cannot retrieve lost progress. Avoid deleting the app as a troubleshooting step if you want to keep your game. Closing and reopening the app or restarting your device is a safer first step. Device backups and transfers depend on your Apple settings.

Sound, music, and accessibility

Sound effects and music start switched off. You can enable them in Settings. Gameplay preferences also let you adjust haptics, animation, and region patterns. Your iPhone's sound settings may affect playback.

Purchases and advertising

Levels 1–100, Daily Orbit and Weekly Supernova stay free. One optional Full Game Unlock permanently unlocks every level from 101 onward, including levels added in future updates for the price Apple shows before purchase ($1.99 US base price). Optional hint and reveal packs (Hint Pack, Reveal Pack, Star Bundle, Galaxy Bundle) are consumable: they are added to this device's stash and used up when spent. Restore Purchases does not re-add consumed or locally lost hints and reveals. You also earn 1 hint and 1 reveal for every 100 completed levels and every 7-day streak. There are no subscriptions or advertisements. Stardust is earned in the game and has no cash value.

To restore an existing Full Game Unlock, use Settings → Restore Purchases, or Restore Purchases on the unlock screen, with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Restoring access does not restore local puzzle progress. A pending approval does not charge again or unlock until Apple confirms it. For payment or refund help, use Apple purchase support.

Game Center (v1.0.1)

Open Galaxy Archive and tap the trophy to connect to Apple Game Center and share badge progress. Existing earned badges are included. If sign-in or syncing fails, keep playing and try again later. Use the adjacent Game Center options menu to disconnect and stop future reporting. Existing Apple achievements remain on your Game Center profile. Game Center does not restore campaign progress or purchases.

Privacy and terms

The app works offline and does not collect your gameplay or personal information. If you email us, we receive the message and the contact details you provide. Read the Privacy Policy for the full explanation, including website hosting, or review the Terms of Use.
