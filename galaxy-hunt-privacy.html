Galaxy Hunt! Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026 · App version 1.1.0

Galaxy Hunt! is a logic puzzle game provided by Victor Lin ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains the current app's privacy practices and separately describes what happens if you email support or visit our website.

1. The app does not collect your data

Galaxy Hunt! puzzles work offline. New purchases and purchase restores may require a connection to Apple. It does not send your gameplay, personal information, or device identifiers to us or to an analytics service. There are no developer-operated accounts, advertisements, tracking technologies, analytics SDKs, or advertising identifiers in the app. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

The app offers one optional, permanent Full Game Unlock for every campaign level from 101 onward, including levels added in future updates. Apple processes purchases and restores through StoreKit. The app checks Apple-verified purchase entitlement information on your device to provide access; it does not transmit your purchase history or payment information to us. We do not receive your payment card details. Optional consumable hint and ability packs are also processed by Apple; the app records the credited amounts and transaction identifiers only on your device so a transaction is never credited twice. There are no subscriptions or third-party purchase SDKs. Puzzles and audio are bundled with the app; the game does not download them from a server.

2. Information saved on your device

To remember your game, Galaxy Hunt! saves the following in local app preferences:

  • Completed levels and the last level played.
  • The current puzzle attempt, including cell marks, incorrect checks, assistance used, and elapsed playing time.
  • Daily Orbit completion dates and Weekly Supernova completion weeks.
  • Earned achievements and the flawless-completion count.
  • Unlocked and equipped cosmetic items.
  • Gameplay, accessibility, sound, and music preferences, and whether you enabled Game Center achievement reporting.

Badges, streaks, and Stardust balances may be calculated from these records. These records provide the game on your device. If you choose to connect Game Center, achievement identifiers and completion percentages derived from these records are also sent directly to Apple as described below. We do not receive them, and there is no developer-operated account, cloud save, or cross-device synchronization service.

Your operating system may include app data in a device backup or transfer according to your Apple settings. We do not operate or have access to those backups. Apple's services are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Optional Apple Game Center

Starting in version 1.1.0, you can choose to connect Apple Game Center from Galaxy Archive. When connected, the app sends achievement identifiers and completion percentages directly to Apple, including badges already earned on this device. Apple associates this progress with your signed-in Game Center profile and may display it according to your Apple settings. We do not operate a server for this feature or receive these reports. The app does not request your friends, email address or profile name, and does not persist your Game Center player identifier.

Connecting is optional. Puzzles, local badges and progress continue to work without Game Center or an internet connection. Pending progress is retried from local records when the service becomes available. You can stop future reporting using Galaxy Archive → Game Center options → Disconnect Game Center. Disconnecting or deleting this app does not delete achievements already held by Apple. Manage your Game Center account and privacy choices through Apple's settings. Apple's processing is governed by Game Center & Privacy. Game Center achievement reporting does not provide cloud saves or restore local campaign progress.

3. Retention and removal of local data

Local game records remain until the app replaces them during normal play or you remove the app and its data. Deleting the app through iOS Settings removes its local app data; offloading the app retains it. A later device restore may restore a backup copy. Manage backups separately in your Apple settings if you also want to remove those copies.

We cannot view, export, restore, or remotely delete your on-device progress. There is no app account to delete.

4. If you contact support

If you choose to email mickey18labs@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments or details you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request, troubleshoot the issue, and maintain the related support correspondence. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Our email provider processes this correspondence to deliver and store email. Support information is kept only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to delete or correct support correspondence at the same address. We do not use it for advertising.

5. If you visit our website

The Galaxy Hunt! privacy, terms, and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting a website necessarily sends technical request information, such as your IP address, requested page, and browser information, to its host. GitHub may process that information to serve and secure the site under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

These Galaxy Hunt! pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or cookies set by our page code. Website hosting requests and voluntary support emails are separate from offline gameplay. Following links to other websites or opening your email app is subject to those services' privacy practices.

6. Children

Galaxy Hunt! is a general-audience puzzle game. Its app collects no personal information from children or other players and contains no developer-operated chat or social profiles, or advertising. Optional Game Center uses Apple’s profile and privacy settings. A parent or guardian should contact support on a child's behalf. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in support correspondence, contact us so we can address it and delete it where appropriate.

7. Your choices and rights

You can play without creating an account or contacting us. You control the app's local records through your device, as described above. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information you voluntarily send to support, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Email us to exercise applicable rights. We cannot identify or retrieve local game data because it is not sent to us.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if our practices change. If a future app version introduces a new data practice, we will describe it before it takes effect and obtain consent where required. Planned features are not described as existing data collection in this policy.
